## Services

- **Accredited training:** Coordination of access to relevant accredited programmes through appropriately accredited provider partners, within their approved scope.
- **Non-accredited training:** Customised workplace learning and awareness programmes designed around organisational needs.
- **Corporate training solutions:** Training needs discussions, in-house and on-site arrangements, learner coordination, project management, evaluation and reporting.
- **Occupational health and safety training:** Workplace safety awareness, hazard identification and risk assessment, incident investigation and health and safety representative awareness, subject to trainer competence and programme requirements.
- **Retail and workplace skills:** Customer service, retail operations, supervisory development, communication, teamwork, conflict management, workplace ethics and professional conduct.
- **Skills development support:** Selected SDF and SETA-related administrative support, including WSP and ATR assistance, where agreed with the client. This is a supplementary service, not the core business.

## Delivery formats

Face-to-face classroom training, on-site workplace training, virtual sessions, blended learning and practical workshops. Each programme is scoped with the client to agree learning outcomes, delivery arrangements, learner requirements, assessment arrangements (where applicable), records and reporting.
