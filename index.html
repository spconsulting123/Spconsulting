Frequently asked questions


What training does SP Consulting offer?
Accredited and non-accredited training, corporate training solutions, occupational health and safety training, retail and workplace skills, and selected SDF and SETA administration support.


Is the training accredited?
We coordinate access to accredited programmes through appropriately accredited provider partners, within their approved scope. We also offer non-accredited workplace learning and awareness programmes.


How is training delivered?
Face-to-face classroom training, on-site workplace training, virtual sessions, blended learning and practical workshops. Our own training venue is still to be confirmed.


Who do you work with?
Businesses, public-sector organisations, NGOs and training-provider partners.


Can you help with WSP and ATR submissions?
Yes, we provide selected SDF and SETA-related administrative support, including WSP and ATR assistance, where agreed with the client.


Where are you based?
Athlone, Cape Town, in the Western Cape, South Africa.
